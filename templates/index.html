Who We Are

Abdulla bin Mohamed Al falasi

A newly set up audit and accounting firm which provides a wide range of high-Quality financial services ( Audit, accounting, tax, consulting and business advisory) by a group of dedicated young talented professionals who delivers the best valuable clients in timely and cost-effective manner, The team has been working together for more than 10 years and has now formed AMF.

  • We are an existing team working under a new name.
  • Our strength is our team spirit, experience and co- existence.

  • We are flexible, we adapt quickly to client needs and deliver on time high quality deliverables.
  • We help you applying and implementing new and revised IFRSs.

  • We are up to date with tax and other regulatory changes or amends.
